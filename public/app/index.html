The Standardized 4-Criteria Rubric

Learn how InterviewAce grades each answer on a fixed 20-mark scale.

Criterion 1

Correctness & Technical Accuracy (0–5 Marks)

Assesses factual correctness, algorithmic accuracy, edge-case handling, and conceptual mastery. Zero credit is awarded for incorrect or hallucinated technical assertions.

Criterion 2

Relevance & Direct Answers (0–5 Marks)

Evaluates whether the candidate directly addressed the specific prompt without deflection, waffle, or generic filler. Direct, on-point responses receive top marks.

Criterion 3

Clarity & Logical Structure (0–5 Marks)

Measures communication effectiveness, structured explanations (such as STAR method for behavioral questions or approach-code-complexity for coding), and clear terminology.

Criterion 4

Completeness & Depth (0–5 Marks)

Evaluates thoroughness, discussion of trade-offs (time vs. space complexity, relational vs. NoSQL), scaling constraints, and practical real-world considerations.
